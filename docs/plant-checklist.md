# Plant checklist

All 30 plants in `backend/data/fl_recommendations.json`. The app only recommends a plant after its `"verified"` field is changed from `false` to `true`.

**How to check a plant**

1. **Native ✓:** open the USF Atlas species page. It should say the plant is native to Florida. (Firebush, South Florida slash pine and dune sunflower link to the variety we recommend; check the page matches the "ask for" name.)
2. **Palm Beach ✓:** on the Atlas map, Palm Beach County should be shaded for this plant.
3. **Facts ✓:** open each source link and confirm it says what the fact says.
4. **Pairing ✓:** the "Replaces" suggestions are EcoLoco's own picks, not from a source. Check that the plant would fit where the invasive grows (similar size and sun). Write any doubts in Notes.
5. When all four are ticked, change `"verified": false` to `"verified": true` for that plant in the JSON file.
